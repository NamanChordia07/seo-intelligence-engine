# Looking for an OctaFX Alternative in India? Start With the Legal Options

> **Risk warning:** CFDs and leveraged forex products are complex instruments and come with a high risk of losing money rapidly due to leverage. Only trade with money you can afford to lose. This content is educational and is not investment advice or an offer to trade.

Searches for "OctaFX alternative" rose sharply after reports of enforcement action in India. Many of these searchers want to know two things: what happened, and where they can trade instead. Swapping one unauthorised offshore platform for another would not solve the underlying problem, so this guide summarises what has been reported, sets out what Indian rules permit, and gives a checklist for evaluating any alternative.[^1]

**Key takeaways**

- Enforcement actions have been reported; the company disputes the allegations.
- The meaningful alternative is a legally permitted route, not another offshore app.
- Verify the legal entity, its regulator and its Indian registration status before moving money.

## What has been reported

India's Directorate of Enforcement (ED) has been investigating OctaFX-linked entities under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act and has issued provisional attachment orders.[^2] In July 2025 it was reported that the ED attached assets in Spain, including a yacht and residences, worth about Rs 131 crore.[^3] In October 2025 a further provisional attachment of cryptocurrency worth about Rs 2,385 crore was reported.[^4]

Octa has publicly rejected the allegations against it and has said it is not affiliated with the individual named by investigators.[^5] Provisional attachment is a step in an investigation, not a final court verdict; this page will be refreshed as official information changes.

## Why 'just switch broker' is the wrong question

RBI's position applies to every platform, not only one brand: residents may undertake forex transactions only with authorised persons and on authorised electronic trading platforms or recognised stock exchanges.[^1] RBI also cautions that an entity not appearing on its Alert List should not be assumed to be authorised.[^1] Residents who send money for unauthorised forex transactions may face penal action under FEMA.[^6]

So the useful alternative is not a different offshore app - it is a route that is permitted for Indian residents. For the full legal picture, see [whether forex trading is legal in India](/hi-in/guides/bharat-mein-forex-trading-legal-hai-kya).

## Legal alternatives for Indian residents

- **Exchange-traded currency derivatives:** recognised Indian exchanges offer currency futures and options in permitted pairs - USD-INR, EUR-INR, GBP-INR, JPY-INR and the crosses EUR-USD, GBP-USD and USD-JPY.[^6] NSE, for example, runs a currency derivatives segment.[^7]
- **Through a SEBI-registered broker:** access to exchange-traded products is through registered intermediaries, whose registration you can check on SEBI's official lists.[^8]
- **RBI-authorised ETPs:** RBI publishes the list of electronic trading platforms it has authorised.[^6]

We do not recommend or rank individual brokers on this page. Exchange-traded products still carry market risk, and leveraged positions can lose money quickly.

## Checklist before you move money anywhere

1. Identify the exact legal entity you would contract with and its regulator.
2. Check RBI's Alert List and authorised ETP list, and the SEBI registration of any Indian broker.[^8]
3. Refuse any deposit request to a personal bank account or individual UPI ID.
4. Walk away from promises of fixed or guaranteed returns - they are a classic warning sign.
5. Read withdrawal terms before depositing; our [withdrawal guide](/en-in/guides/forex-broker-se-paisa-withdraw-kaise-kare) explains common delays.
6. Keep records of every transaction and communication.

## If you still have money on a platform under investigation

Do not pay anyone who offers to "recover" funds for a fee - this is a frequent follow-on scam. Keep statements, screenshots and emails. Concerns about unauthorised entities can be shared on RBI's Sachet portal[^9], and financial fraud can be reported on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal or helpline 1930.[^10] For legal questions about your specific situation, consult a qualified professional.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is OctaFX banned in India?

We do not characterise the legal outcome; investigations and provisional attachments have been reported, and Octa rejects the allegations.[^5] Separately, RBI permits residents to trade forex only on authorised platforms or recognised exchanges.[^1]

### What is a legal alternative to OctaFX in India?

Exchange-traded currency derivatives in permitted pairs on recognised Indian exchanges, accessed through a SEBI-registered broker.[^6]

### Can I trade EUR/USD legally in India?

RBI's FAQ lists EUR-USD among the cross pairs permitted as exchange-traded currency derivatives on recognised Indian exchanges.[^6]

### Should I pay a fund-recovery service?

No - fee-based recovery offers are a common follow-on scam. Report fraud at cybercrime.gov.in or helpline 1930.[^10]
